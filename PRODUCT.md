Personal site for Keshav Garg, an AI engineer for regulated industries (owner-chosen positioning, 2026-10-20; "Independent" dropped as untrue and generic). ## Product Principles
1. Proof before claims: outcomes and real artifacts lead; adjectives follow.
2. Production, not prototypes: every piece shown shipped or is labelled otherwise.
3. Discretion is part of the offer: sectors and regions, never client names.
4. One person, whole system: the site should feel authored by one engineer, not an agency.

## Brand Commitments
- Owner rejects: flashy/startup aesthetics (gradients, glows, gimmicks), stiff corporate-consultancy tone, hacker/terminal dark-mode "dev portfolio" look, and empty minimalism that says nothing.
- Voice: direct, first person, concrete ("I don't ship demos").
- His name leads, always. Owner rejected (2026-10-20) an initials "designation" mark ("KG 2026"), a full-bleed red band, and standards-document jargon as tacky. No invented marks, no loud color fields, no costume labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
